## Food-delivery-delay-analysis
Excel analysis of food delivery data to find causes of late deliveries, with a dashboard and business recommendations

## Food Delivery Delay Analysis (Excel)

## Problem
A food delivery company faces late deliveries that lead to complaints, refunds, and fewer repeat orders. Management has no clear view of what drives the delays.

## Tools
Microsoft Excel (data cleaning, helper columns, analysis tables, charts, dashboard)

## Dataset
Public food delivery dataset from Kaggle (link: add the dataset URL here)

## Key Findings
- Heavy traffic: 50.6% of orders late vs 7.9% in low traffic
- Evening (5-8 PM) is the busiest window with a 43% late rate vs 9% in the morning
- Motorcycles have the highest late rate (33.9%)
- Fog and cloudy weather (43.8% late) cause more delays than storms

## Recommendations
- Increase rider staffing during evening hours
- Use traffic-aware delivery time estimates
- Treat fog and low-visibility conditions with the same caution as storms
